# v1.0.0 residual risks

- Evidence is synthetic and proves bounded mechanics, not the correctness of any real filing.
- There is no live SEC/EDGAR adapter, Inline XBRL renderer, taxonomy package resolver or correction feed.
- Caller-supplied mappings and ratio policies are not authoritative GAAP/IFRS definitions.
- Reconciliation is structural decimal arithmetic, not audit evidence or accounting assurance.
- Exact-identity selection does not establish economic comparability across entities or periods.
- SHA-256 detects changed bytes but does not authenticate the publisher; artifacts are not signed.
- Only CPython 3.11–3.13 on GitHub-hosted Ubuntu is continuously verified.
- Local files are not a governed warehouse; access control, encryption, retention, telemetry, high
  availability and disaster recovery are outside scope.

Statement Lens is educational software, not financial advice, an accounting opinion or a production
financial-reporting control.
